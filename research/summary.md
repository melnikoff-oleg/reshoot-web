## Offer strategy

The page applies the value equation from the supplied *$100M Offers* PDF:

`(Dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort and sacrifice)`

- Dream outcome: confidence and a safe foundation before the first range trip.
- Perceived likelihood: championship credentials, 8,500+ trainees, social scale,
  original learner videos, and a concrete 36/71/20 curriculum.
- Time delay: instant access and a useful 10–15 minute first lesson tonight.
- Effort/sacrifice: phone-friendly self-paced learning and no firearm required to
  begin.

No new product, artificial discount, countdown, guarantee, or invented result
was introduced.

## Stripe and safety handoff

Stripe currently lists firearms and weapons among restricted businesses that
may require additional due diligence and explicit approval. The product here is
digital education rather than a physical firearm sale, but the account should
be described truthfully and reviewed with Stripe before routing new direct
checkout links. Sources: [Stripe restricted businesses](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses)
and [Stripe restricted-business FAQ](https://support.stripe.com/questions/prohibited-and-restricted-businesses-list-faqs).

The page and terms now state that:

- the service is adult-only, safety-focused digital education;
- no firearms, ammunition, regulated parts, or physical products are sold;
- course access is not a firearm license, professional certification, legal
  advice, or a substitute for lawful in-person supervision;
- live-fire practice must occur only where lawful and properly supervised;
- refund language follows the existing detailed terms rather than the older
  conflicting global 14-day FAQ claim.

Until processor approval and end-to-end checkout testing are complete, the CTA
fallback remains the current ReShoot login flow.
